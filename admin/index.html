DPDP Act (India) Compliance Summary

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023:

  • ✓ Explicit, granular consent is obtained before collecting visitor contact information.
  • ✓ Checkboxes are unchecked by default (no pre-tick / passive opt-ins).
  • ✓ Site visit bookings require two-step Email OTP verification to ensure identity validation.
  • ✓ Consent timestamps and lead sources are recorded in every submission.
