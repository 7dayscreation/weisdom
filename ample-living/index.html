Ample Living

Ample LIVING reflects the balance between space, light, and lifestyle. The name represents openness,
comfort, and well-planned living, creating residences that support everyday ease and long-term well-being.

Arrival Experience

A quiet transition into the residential environment, designed with clarity and proportion. The entry creates a welcoming threshold that sets the tone for the residences beyond.

Elevated Living

A residential building defined by clear architectural lines and balanced proportions. The architecture emphasizes natural light, openness, and thoughtful planning.

A Calm Garden Plaza

A serene landscaped garden with a tranquil outdoor court designed for relaxation and community interaction.

51% Green & Open Spaces

Over half of the site is thoughtfully reserved for landscaped gardens and open areas, creating a balanced, nature-led environment for the development.

Experience Ample Living on Ground

Experience the project at ground level and discover its architecture, spaces, landscape, materials, and surrounding area benefits.

Views Details

The site planning of Ample Living is based on climate-responsive orientation, efficient block placement, and integrated open spaces. Buildings are arranged to maximize daylight, ventilation, and visual openness, while landscape and zoning create a structured yet peaceful residential environment.

Integrated Ground Level Development

The ground level integrates access, amenities, and landscaped spaces to form a functional and welcoming base for the development. Clear zoning ensures efficient circulation, active community areas, and green buffers, creating a well-balanced connection between built form and open spaces.

Shared Spaces for Community Living

A series of shared courts, play areas, and landscaped pockets create everyday spaces where residents can meet, interact, and build a sense of community.

Sectional Planning Strategy

The sectional arrangement places duplex residences along the road-facing entry edge of the site, supported by landscaped buffers at the front and sides to enhance privacy, visual quality, and environmental comfort. The 4BHK apartments are oriented toward the landscaped interior zones, benefiting from quieter surroundings and open views. This planning approach creates a smooth transition from the active site frontage to a calm residential environment.

Unit Configuration & Spatial Planning

The axonometric representation showcases the spatial logic of 4 BHK and duplex units, emphasizing thoughtful planning, proportion, and the relationship between private and shared spaces.

The front-facing duplex units and rear 4 BHK residences are unified through a composed elevation, where projecting balconies and layered massing define the architectural character.
